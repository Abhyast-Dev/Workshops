More than a one-off session

Tailored experiences. Meaningful measurement. Continued learning.

No two schools: or learning groups: are identical. We tailor the workshop to the audience and use structured measurement of knowledge, attitudes and observable or reported behaviours to inform reflection, school insights and practical next steps.

01 / BEFORE

Establish a baseline

Abhyast establishes a relevant baseline through questions on knowledge, attitudes and responses to scenarios.

02 / EXPERIENCE

Explore together

Learners investigate situations, engage with peers and plan responses.

03 / AFTER

Assess understanding

Knowledge is scored against defined criteria; attitude and behavioural indicators are interpreted using appropriate measures, not treated as knowledge grades.

04 / REFLECT

Make thinking visible

Students complete an ungraded self-reflection sheet and may voluntarily share ideas in class.

05 / INSIGHTS

Inform next steps

Schools receive an AI-assisted, professionally reviewed summary of evidence, limitations and suggested follow-up actions.

Abhyast independently designs workshop assessments. Schools authorize participation and agreed data-handling arrangements; individual assessment questions do not require teacher approval.

Included in the engagement

A workshop the school can learn from

Measurement is central: not an optional add-on. Abhyast helps schools understand what learners know, what attitudes they express and how they say they would respond, while identifying what needs follow-up beyond a single workshop.

  • Knowledge: scored responses on selected concepts and applied scenarios, before and after the session.
  • Attitudes: responses that indicate beliefs, perceptions and readiness to consider safer choices: reported descriptively rather than graded as right or wrong.
  • Behaviour: scenario-based decisions and, where feasible, separately agreed observations or later follow-up; intentions and self-reports are not treated as proof of actual behaviour.
  • School Learning Insights Report: observed group patterns, possible misconceptions, and practical reinforcement suggestions.
  • Student Self-Reflection Sheet: a space for students to examine their reasoning and what they would do differently.
  • Shared dialogue: voluntary classroom discussion that helps students test their ideas respectfully.

Results describe the evidence gathered; a single workshop cannot establish lasting behaviour change or diagnose individual learners. Student privacy and required permissions are addressed before delivery.

Learning shared with the community

I Am My Best Friend

A student-led celebration of learning and awareness.

For schools choosing an extended workshop programme, the learning journey can culminate in “I Am My Best Friend”: an event where students actively demonstrate what they have understood and share practical awareness messages with peers, teachers and the school community.

Students might present scenarios, explain and challenge safeguards, facilitate discussions, create posters or perform short role-plays. The format is shaped by the students’ age, the workshop theme and the school’s context.

Participation and presentation are supportive and age-appropriate; students choose what personal reflections, if any, to share. Event scope, audience, supervision and permissions are agreed with the school in advance.

From insight to action

A learning journey that continues

  1. Explore: Engage in relevant real-life situations.
  2. Examine: Assess knowledge and responses.
  3. Reflect: Consider what changed and what remains uncertain.
  4. Demonstrate: Communicate safeguards and decisions creatively.
  5. Spread awareness: Invite others to think critically and act safely.

The showcase is an optional culmination of a tailored engagement, not an automatic inclusion in a single workshop fee.

Our learning philosophy

Students learn to examine a situation: not merely repeat a rule.

Our TAKECARE approach invites learners to Think, Ask, Know, Engage, Confirm, Assess, Reflect and Evaluate. In cyber safety, students investigate vulnerabilities, plan safeguards, challenge their plans and evaluate safety.

About Abhyast

Educational expertise. Tailor-made workshops.

Abhyast Private Limited designs context-sensitive educational experiences that nurture metacognition, or thinking about thinking, and autonomy, or ownership of learning. Psychometric thinking, meaningful evidence and reflection support this focus.

Workshops are tailor-made for the participating school, age group and learning priorities, and facilitated by professionals with relevant subject expertise and practical experience. Abhyast’s educational leadership includes an M.Phil. in Education professional with a specialization in psychometry and educational measurement, who has contributed as an NCERT resource person in AI in Education and Cyber Safety and Security. This background informs the design of assessments and learning experiences, with measurement of knowledge, attitudes and behaviour-related indicators central to the approach.

Abhyast's workshops are independent offerings and are not represented as NCERT-approved or NCERT-endorsed programmes.
